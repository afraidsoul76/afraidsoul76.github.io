Glowline – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · Developer: Bairagi Developers

This policy explains what information the mobile game Glowline (package com.bairagidevelopers.glowline) uses and why. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

1. Information we do not collect

Glowline has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints, daily-puzzle streak, selected theme) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, reminders) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or using Settings → Reset progress deletes them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glowline is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad occasionally between levels, and an optional video you can choose to watch for a hint. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Glowline schedules a local daily reminder on your device ("Your daily puzzle is ready"). No data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders or in your device's notification settings.

5. Technical diagnostics

Glowline is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited technical information, such as crash reports, device model and operating-system version, to keep the engine working. See Unity's player privacy policy.

6. Children

Glowline is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

7. Security and retention

We do not hold your data. Data processed by Google AdMob or Unity is handled under their policies linked above. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
